// ABOUT US

A small studio
with a dark heart

Founded in 2026, DoubleCup Studios is a two-person indie studio from Brazil, born where Information Technology meets a lifelong passion for games.

One of us builds the game solo — code, systems and gameplay. The other gives it a face, designing its characters and world.

Together we are crafting a low poly dark fantasy: simple shapes, dense atmosphere and solid mechanics.
